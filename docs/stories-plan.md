## The text

As he wrote it. The vowel marks he had left out were added on the title, page 7 (`جَبَلِ`), page 8 (`ظَلَّتْ تَسْعَى`, `أَشْوَاطٍ`), page 10 (`سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ`) and page 13 (`زُرْتَ مَكَّةَ وَشَرِبْتَ`).

There was one grammar fix: he wrote «سَبْعَ أشواط», and it is now «سَبْعَةَ أَشْوَاطٍ». شَوْط is masculine, so the number from three to ten takes the feminine form. «سَبْعَ مَرَّاتٍ» is right only because مَرَّة is feminine. Tell Claude if you want it back.

The honorifics stand where he wrote them: at the first mention of Ismail, Ibrahim, Hajar (page 1) and Jibreel (page 9), and once more on the last page, «هَاجَرَ وَإِسْمَاعِيلَ عَلَيْهِمَا السَّلَامُ», which he added before recording. He is not making a general rule yet, but saying them every time is too much for a child. The data file is `public/stories/zamzam.json`. Edit it only with the parents' say.

## Recordings to make

Use `record.html`, on the PC: 14 cards in the group "Story: Ismail and Zamzam". The cards read their text from the story file, so they always say what the screen says.

- **Clips:** `zamzam-title` and `zamzam-1` to `zamzam-13`, in Fusha only. The words light up as they are spoken, so the voice must say exactly the words on screen. The dialect switch does not apply to stories.
- **How to read:** slowly and warmly, with a short breath at each comma and full stop. The automatic timing finds those pauses and keeps the ink in step.
- **Afterwards:** Claude adds the files to `FILES` in `sw.js`. Then `timing.html` should learn to open story pages, so that a page whose ink runs ahead or behind can be tapped.

## What the sources say

These are notes for the parents, from sunnah.com and the Shamela connector.

- **The angel and the water.** Sahih al-Bukhari 3364 (https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3364) says the angel "dug with his heel, or he said: with his wing". Ibn al-Mulaqqin explains that "the apparent meaning is that it is the narrator's doubt" ([التوضيح لشرح الجامع الصحيح ١٩/ ٣٩٦](https://shamela.ws/book/13252/11544)). Bukhari 3365 (https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3365) names Jibreel and says he pressed his heel into the ground. Nobody flew. The father's «فَضَرَبَ الْأَرْضَ» fits both narrations.
- **Hajar's words.** In the hadith she asked «آللَّهُ الَّذِي أَمَرَكَ بِهَذَا؟» and said «إِذًا لَا يُضَيِّعُنَا». The father's page 4 retells this.
- **The water.** It ran out and "she was thirsty and her son was thirsty" (3364).
- **The Kaaba.** There was no Kaaba then. The House was "raised from the ground like a hillock" (3364), and Ibrahim and Ismail built it years later. So the Kaaba appears only on the last page, as it is today.
- **Zamzam and du'a.** «مَاءُ زَمْزَمَ لِمَا شُرِبَ لَهُ», Sunan Ibn Majah 3062 (https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:3062), graded Hasan by Darussalam.
