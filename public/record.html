Recording tool

Admin tool, not linked from the site. Each card is one clip the site plays. Where the child hears a prompt in either dialect there are two cards: the Fusha one, and the Egyptian one named -masri, whose text the child sees, exactly as written, whenever that recording plays: record it as the card reads, or tell Claude the change first. Press Record (or R), wait a beat, say it, press Stop. The newest take is kept; keep an older one if it was better. Every take stays on this computer, even after a reload, until you discard it. Then Write to folder: the first time, choose the project's public/audio folder (Chrome or Edge on a computer); after that the page remembers it. A new recording of a name that already has an .m4a file is ignored by the site until that .m4a is removed.

Before a sitting, to hear how noisy the room is. Nothing is saved.

Aim for the shaded band: peaks around −12 dBFS. Above −1 dBFS the bar turns red.

Keys: R record or stop · Space play or pause the card's kept take · N next card · P previous card · Esc stop playing
