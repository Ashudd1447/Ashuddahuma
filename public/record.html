Recording tool

Parents' tool, not linked from the site. Each card is one clip the site plays. Press Record, wait a beat, say it, press Stop. The newest take is kept; keep an older one if it was better. Then Write to folder and choose the public/audio folder of the repo (Chrome or Edge on a computer), or download each take. A new recording of a name that already has an .m4a file is ignored by the site until that .m4a is removed.

Aim for the voice to reach between the two marks (−18 to −6 dBFS). Press R to record or stop the selected card.
